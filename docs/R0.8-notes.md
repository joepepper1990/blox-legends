# R0.8 "Learning Core": build notes

What was built against the Revision 3.1 plan, and the places where testing led to a different rule. Every change below has a test.

## Changes from the plan, and why

| Plan | Built | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Applied:** at least 3 right transfer answers across at least 2 worlds | Over the last 8 transfer answers, rights minus twice the wrongs must reach 4. The rights must also come from enough worlds. | The simulations showed a guesser could collect 3 lucky right answers among many wrong ones. With the new rule a guesser gets there about 1 time in 22, the same as passing a check by luck. Someone right 85% of the time gets there after about 7 answers. |
| **Applied in 2 worlds** for every skill | Word problems (`m.wp1`) and views from above (`s.top`) need 1 other world for now (`appliedMechs: 1`, listed in the registry test). | A story can't fit on a Glass Bridge sign. A view from above needs a 3D build, and Vault doors draw builds flat. |
| **≤ 2 checks/reviews per run** | One 3-question check per run, up to 2 reviews and 1 misconception recheck. | A check is 3 questions and is clearer finished in one run. Rechecks had crowded out reviews in the simulations. |
| **Column methods** with the Number zone on the Glass Bridge | Column addition and subtraction live in the Code Vault. | A written sum needs a door or board; it doesn't fit on glass. |
| **Rapid guess** below the format's minimum time | Same on the Vault and the Lab. On the Glass Bridge, only an answer faster than the quickest possible jump (1.1 s, checked against the level bot) counts as rapid. | A jump can't be told apart from thinking time, so a fluent child must not be marked as guessing. |
| **Glass Bridge rows** of 2–3 panels | Always 3 panels. | Two-way guesses are too lucky to count as evidence. |
| **Pictures of builds** on doors and signs | Each build is turned to a view where no cube is hidden, unless it holds another cube up. | A flat picture can hide a cube. Turning a build never changes the answer. |
| **Term Tracker:** expected objectives by how far through the term it is | Expected objectives by how much of the term he has been playing. | A child who starts mid-term shouldn't show as "behind" on the first day. |

## Found and fixed by the tests

- **Code banks:** distractors that were secretly right, for example cowgirl, lightbulb, postcard, glad/proud and little/young.
- **Prefixes:** 30 "wrong" prefixes that make real words, for example unpossible, inlock, misbuild and discount. The wrong prefixes now come from a 275,000-word dictionary.
- **Misspellings that are real words:** calender, belive, compleat, enuf, strang and others.
- **Sentences where a second answer also reads naturally:** "I learn to read ___ school" (both "at" and "in" work), "The toys are ___ the box", and others.
- **Mirror codes ran out of words** for a heavy player (the capacity rule). The code word lists grew from 363 to 1,283 common words.
- **A race:** the opening tip could cover the explanation of a mistake. This was found on CI's slower machine.
